Key Figures in Emergency Mapping

John Snow
1813 – 1858
Used a dot map to identify the source of London's 1854 cholera outbreak — the first use of spatial analysis in emergency response.
Roger Tomlinson
1933 – 2014
Invented the Geographic Information System in the 1960s, making it possible to layer spatial data for disaster planning and response.
Jack Dangermond
1945 – Present
Founded Esri and developed ArcGIS, putting real-time GIS tools in the hands of emergency managers across more than 200 countries.
