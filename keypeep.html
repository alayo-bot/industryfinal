Key Figures in Emergency Mapping

John Snow
1813 – 1858
First used a dot map to trace London's 1854 cholera outbreak — the origin of spatial analysis in emergency response.
Roger Tomlinson
1933 – 2014
Invented GIS in the 1960s, enabling emergency planners to layer and analyze spatial data for the first time.
Jack Dangermond
1945 – Present
Founded Esri and built ArcGIS, making real-time GIS accessible to emergency managers in over 200 countries.
